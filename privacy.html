Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date published]

This policy explains what happens to your data when you visit or purchase Temperament, a product of Curran Crafts, operated by Matthew Curran (“we,” “us,” “our”).

The short version

Temperament collects almost nothing about you. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no forms anywhere in the App. The only two things that touch your data are a cookieless analytics tool and your own browser's local storage, both described below. Payment information is handled entirely by Paddle, our reseller, and never reaches us directly.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing identifying. Temperament has no login, no user accounts, and no tracking of who you are.

Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they open. This service is cookieless: it sets no cookies, requires no consent banner under current regulation, and does not build a profile of individual visitors. It reports aggregate traffic only.

Local storage. To remember your place, such as the last panel you had open or your Quiz progress, Temperament stores a small amount of data directly in your own browser (via localStorage). This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us, and is cleared if you clear your browser's site data.

What Paddle collects

If you purchase a license, your order is processed by Paddle.com, acting as the Merchant of Record. Paddle collects the information necessary to complete your purchase, such as your name, email address, and payment details, and handles that information under its own privacy notice, available at paddle.com/legal. We receive only what's needed to fulfill your order and provide support, such as your name, email, and which license you purchased.

Children's privacy

Temperament is built with university and conservatory-level students in mind and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to matthew@matthewcurran.net.
