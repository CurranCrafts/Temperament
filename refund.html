Individual license ($19)

Studio, Department, and Institution licenses

Because these licenses include setup work on our side (a private deployment, a signed license agreement, and in some tiers a live session with faculty or students), refund terms are as follows:

  • Within 14 days of purchase, before your private deployment is delivered: full refund.
  • Within 14 days of purchase, after delivery: full refund minus a $75 setup fee.
  • After 14 days: no refund for the current license term, but you're welcome to let the license lapse and not renew.

If we've made an error, such as billing you incorrectly or delivering a broken product, we'll make it right regardless of these windows.

How refunds are processed

All payments are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle handles the mechanics of issuing an approved refund back to your original payment method. Refund requests should still come to us first at matthew@matthewcurran.net so we can confirm the license is being cancelled.
