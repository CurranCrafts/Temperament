# Temperament

**The Nature of Music.** Music theory you can see and hear.

Temperament is a hands-on music theory companion for serious young musicians. It is built on a piano keyboard and runs on the phone they already carry, so a student can tap a key, see where it sits in the circle of fifths, and hear what it does. It was made first for undergraduate music majors, then for jazz majors, and it is open to any curious amateur who wants to know why music works the way it does.

There are nine sections. The circle of fifths. Keys and chords. The modes. Ear training, with interval and chord-quality lessons and a challenge mode. An eleven-lesson jazz course. The mathematics of the overtone series and temperament, including why twelve perfect fifths never quite close the circle and what we do about it. A history of Western music theory. A 122-question quiz bank. And an about page.
